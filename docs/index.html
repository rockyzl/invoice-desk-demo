    From inbox to ERP draft

    1. Receive the invoiceThe case starts with sample fields from a supplier PDF. A pilot would pull them from your shared inbox.
    2. Read and verifyCompare invoice quantity and price with the order and receipt. The page calculates the difference from the sample values.
    3. Review exceptionsTry requesting a correction and approving the revised invoice as the finance reviewer.
    4. Save the draftThe connected version can save a purchase invoice draft to a local ERPNext sandbox and show its record ID.

    In a client pilot: inbox or upload → document extraction → order and receipt lookup → finance review → draft writeback.
